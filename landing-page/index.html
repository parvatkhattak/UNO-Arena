How to Setup Offline Play

Zero internet required. Just follow these steps to connect with nearby players.

1

Host Sets Up Hotspot

One player turns on their phone's Wi-Fi Hotspot (settings menu). Note: cellular data can remain OFF.

2

Clients Join Hotspot

Other players open Wi-Fi settings on their devices and connect to the host's created network.

3

Connect and Play

Open UNO Arena. Host presses "Host Game" to get room IP. Clients press "Join Game" and type that IP. Play!
